## Answers that are easy to mis-copy

### SB p. 144, 7A

- a: 1 will get, 2 won’t mark, 3 are / ’re, 4 doesn’t improve, 5 hurry, 6 don’t know, 7 shouts, 8 will finish, 9 won’t go out, 10 need
- b: 1 until, 2 before, 3 when, 4 after, 5 unless, 6 as soon as, 7 until, 8 when, 9 unless, 10 before

### SB p. 66, exercise 6b

2 E, 3 G, 4 H, 5 B, 6 D, 7 F, 8 A. Gap 1 is the example (the unused letter is C).

A tiger mother is a very strict mother who makes her children study very hard and doesn’t really allow them any free time. She wants them to be the best in everything.

### WB p. 44, exercise 4

- a: 3 study
- b: 1 F, 2 T, 3 F, 4 F, 5 T, 6 F, 7 F, 8 F

3 is false because the text never says marks are higher than they used to be. 7 is false because the ban is after 10 p.m., not before 9 p.m. 8 is false because the owner is punished and the pupils are sent home.
